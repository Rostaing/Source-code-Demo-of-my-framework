Discover RostaingChain, the ultimate agentic RAG framework designed to put autonomous and secure Artificial Intelligence systems into production with Python!

In this demo, I show you how to bridge the gap between local privacy (Ollama, open-source models) and the power of the Cloud (OpenAI, Groq, DeepSeek, Claude) to create a custom AI assistant. No more AI hallucinations or outdated data—RostaingChain revolutionizes the way you interact with your databases and files.

Key RostaingChain features showcased:

• **Live Watcher (Real-Time):** Modify a file, an SQL row, or a website, and your AI updates its knowledge instantly.
• **Deep Profiling (Anti-Hallucination):** Automatic statistical analysis of your data sources (CSV, Excel, SQL, Pandas DataFrame) for 100% reliable answers.
• **Built-in DLP Security:** Automatic masking of sensitive data (IBANs, emails, salaries, phone numbers, etc.) before display.
• **Native Multi-Modal:** Chat with Text, PDFs, Images, Audio (via Whisper), and even YouTube videos!
• **Universal Sources:** Connect your AI to PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, Neo4j, local files, and the Web.
